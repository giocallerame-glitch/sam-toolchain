Sam — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24 · App: com.pocketcoder.app

Sam is a personal assistant that runs on your phone. We (the developer) do not run a server, do not have an account system, and do not collect or receive your data. This policy explains what stays on your device, what leaves it, and to whom.

What stays on your phone

  • Your content — chats, projects, apps you build, documents, memory, your diary, reminders, gallery, and family letters — is stored only on your device.
  • Your API keys — if you connect an AI provider, its key is stored on the device, encrypted. It is used only to call that provider directly.

What leaves your phone, and when

Nothing leaves your phone unless you use a feature that needs it:

  • Notifications: Sam can read your notifications on the device to build your briefing. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Location: used on the device for maps. To find places or a route, your search and approximate location may be sent to free map services (OpenStreetMap / Nominatim / Photon / Overpass / OSRM) and to Open-Meteo for the briefing's forecast, and — only if you enable it — to Apify for place ratings.
  • Microphone: used for voice and the “Hey Sam” wake word. If you enable the cloud voice, the text being spoken is sent to your voice provider (e.g. ElevenLabs) to produce audio.
  • Camera (“See”): a photo you choose to show is sent to your AI provider (e.g. DeepSeek) to answer your question.
  • AI chat, apps and images: the text you enter is sent to the AI provider you connected (e.g. DeepSeek) to produce the answer; image prompts go to your image provider (e.g. Replicate).
  • Web search: when a question needs current information, the search text is sent to a search provider — DuckDuckGo by default, or Brave Search if you add your own key. Results may link to Wikipedia or Google News; opening one of those links is your tap, not the app's.
  • Weather: for the briefing, your approximate location is sent to Open-Meteo (no account, no key).
  • Purchases: Sam Pro is bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card details.

Which provider receives what is decided by you, using your own accounts and keys.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.
  • We do not collect data from children; the app is a general tool.

Your control

  • Remove a provider key to stop that usage.
  • Clearing the app’s data (or uninstalling) deletes everything stored on the device.
