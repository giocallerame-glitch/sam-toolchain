Sam — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30 · App: com.pocketcoder.app

Sam is a personal assistant that runs on your phone. We (the developer) do not run a server, do not have an account system, and do not collect or receive your data. This policy explains what stays on your device, what leaves it, and to whom.

What stays on your phone

  • Your content — chats, projects, apps you build, documents, memory, your diary, reminders, gallery, and family letters — is stored only on your device.
  • Your API keys — if you connect an AI provider, its key is stored on the device, encrypted. It is used only to call that provider directly.

What leaves your phone, and when

Nothing leaves your phone unless you use a feature that needs it:

  • Notifications: Sam can read your notifications on the device to build your briefing. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Location: used on the device for maps. To find places or a route, your search and your location (the phone’s current fix) may be sent to free map services (OpenStreetMap / Nominatim / Photon / Overpass / OSRM) and to Open-Meteo for the briefing’s forecast. No location history is stored.
  • Microphone: used for voice and the “Hey Sam” wake word. Your speech is turned into text by the phone’s speech recognizer (usually Google’s; on some phones this runs on the device itself); Sam keeps no recording of it. Her spoken replies are generated on your phone by a voice model that is downloaded to the device — the words she says are never sent to a voice service.
  • Camera (“See”): a photo you choose to show is sent to your AI provider (e.g. DeepSeek) to answer your question.
  • “Let me look” after an error: if you tap it, a picture of the app’s own screen is sent to your AI provider so she can explain what went wrong. Only the app’s own window is in it — nothing else running on your phone. She may take at most two such pictures per failure.
  • AI chat, apps and images: the text you enter is sent to the AI provider you connected (e.g. DeepSeek) to produce the answer; image prompts go to your image provider (e.g. Replicate).
  • Calendar: if you turn the calendar connector on, Sam reads event titles and times for the next seven days on the phone. The short “Next: …” line in your briefing is made on the phone; when you chat with the calendar on, those events travel with your message to your AI provider, so she can answer “what’s on today”. Turn it off and nothing is read.
  • Crash reports: if an app you generated crashes, it can send its stack trace to Sam on the phone. Only when you tap the one-tap AI fix is that trace — with the project’s source files — sent to your AI provider (e.g. DeepSeek) to find the bug. Nothing about a crash leaves the phone unless you ask for the fix.
  • Your name: the name you give Sam in Settings is included in the message she sends to your AI provider, so she can talk to you by it. Leave it blank and nothing is added.
  • Web search: when a question needs current information, the search text is sent to your AI provider’s search service (DeepSeek) — the same key and account as the chat. When you paste a link, that page is retrieved and read by the same service. Opening links in an answer is your tap, not the app’s.
  • Weather: for the briefing, your location (the phone’s current fix) is sent to Open-Meteo (no account, no key).
  • Purchases: Sam Pro is bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card details.

Which provider receives what is decided by you, using your own accounts and keys.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.
  • We do not collect data from children; the app is a general tool.

Your control

  • Remove a provider key to stop that usage.
  • Clearing the app’s data (or uninstalling) deletes everything stored on the device.
